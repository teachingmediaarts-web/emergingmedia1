OVERALL SEMESTER THEME - IMAGINE OTHERWISE: A VISUAL MANIFESTO This semester, we will explore how artists and media makers can use visual language to question the world around us and imagine what might exist otherwise. In a time marked by uncertainty, change, and competing visions of the future, how can creative practice help us see beyond what already exists? Throughout the semester, you will develop a series of interconnected projects that build toward a visual manifesto—a body of work that expresses an idea, question, desire, critique, or possibility through image, design, movement, and the web. Your manifesto might respond to a social issue, a personal experience, a cultural condition, or simply something you are curious about. This course is visually oriented and explores how images can shape the way we understand ourselves and the world around us. As we move through digital imaging tools—including Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, HTML, and CSS—you will develop a visual language that communicates an idea, question, desire, or possibility.
