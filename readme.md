COURSE DETAILS
This hands-on production course introduces students to the practical fundamentals of visual studies, design, digital graphics, illustration and web production. It is a foundations course in 2D design, illustration and digital imaging. Students are introduced to a range of media production equipment and techniques, including: The history of design, computer graphics, illustration and new media; Issues in visual studies; Image composition, framing, and layout; Typography and text/image relationships; basic imaging and vector based illustration software.
EXPECTED OUTCOMES
Analyze and distinguish concepts in visual communication, design, computer graphics, illustration, photography, and new media
Explain and discuss contemporary issues in image production and new media
Manipulate raster graphics through the use of compositing, composition, framing, layout
Create vector illustrations and apply design principles to typography and text/image relationships
Create basic animations and motion graphics
Plan and apply a comprehensive understanding of HTML and CSS to web design

What were points of interest and why?

Did you take anything away from the reading - it can be positive or negative.

Regarding AI
All assignments submitted in this class must be your original work and written by you alone. Copying and pasting from the Internet or another student's work without attributing the source is plagiarism, whether intentional or not. While you may use AI tools like ChatGPT or Grammarly for feedback on how to improve your writing, using any AI tools to write your assignments is strictly prohibited and will be treated as plagiarism. Anyone presenting someone else's writing as their own, whether from another student, the Internet or any AI tool generating text that is copied into your work, will receive a 0 for the assignment and will be reported to the Office of Student Conduct.
